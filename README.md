Ten years building software, most of it on **Ruby on Rails** and **React/TypeScript**, with **Go** where it earns its place. I started out as a designer, so I care about how things look as much as how they run.

What I usually get hired for:

- **Shipping MVPs** from a blank repo to a deployed product
- **Rescuing legacy Rails**: old Ruby/Rails upgrades, untangling codebases nobody wants to touch
- **Adding LLM features** to real products, not demos
- **Owning the boring parts**: Docker, CI/CD, monitoring, self-hosted deploys on Hetzner + Coolify

Previously co-ran [Esseal](https://www.esseal.co.uk), a small dev agency, and led two teams at [DeltaLabs](http://deltalabs.io) for four and a half years.
